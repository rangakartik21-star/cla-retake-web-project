Study tips

Small improvements make a big difference.

Consistent habits usually work better than last-minute marathon sessions. Keep the process simple and measurable.

01

Set one clear goal

Decide exactly what you want to complete before you start studying.

02

Remove distractions

Keep unnecessary tabs, notifications and your phone away during focus time.

03

Recall before rereading

Try to remember key ideas from memory first, then check your notes.
