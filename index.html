About the business

A local electronics retailer built around practical buying decisions.

Vimal Electronics is a neighbourhood electronics and home-appliance retailer serving customers around Vastral Road / Amraiwadi in Ahmedabad. The website is designed as a digital front door to the store rather than an online checkout system.

Customers can review major categories, compare the kinds of features that usually matter, prepare an enquiry, and contact the store for the latest price, model and availability.

Key products

Four categories customers often compare carefully.

These are buying-guide illustrations, not claims about any particular model currently in stock. Contact the store for current brands, specifications and pricing.

Smart TV

Think about size, panel and software.

For most homes, the right TV depends on viewing distance, room brightness and how heavily streaming apps are used.

4K resolutionHDR supportSmart appsHDMI ports
  • Measure the viewing distance before choosing screen size.
  • Check panel type and brightness if the room gets strong daylight.
  • Confirm app support and enough HDMI ports for consoles or set-top boxes.

Split AC

Match tonnage and efficiency to the room.

Cooling capacity should fit room size and heat load. Energy efficiency matters most when the AC runs for long hours.

Inverter1-2 ton rangeEnergy ratingAir filtration
  • Room area, sunlight and top-floor heat affect required capacity.
  • Higher efficiency can matter more than a small upfront price difference.
  • Ask about installation requirements and warranty coverage.

Refrigerator

Choose capacity around household size and habits.

Capacity, door configuration and power efficiency should reflect family size, kitchen space and how groceries are stored.

Frost-freeInverter compressor300-400L classConvertible modes
  • Measure the kitchen opening before deciding on a larger refrigerator.
  • Frost-free models reduce manual defrosting.
  • Adjustable storage and convertible zones help flexible households.

Washing Machine

Capacity and loading style shape daily convenience.

Family size, wash frequency and available space usually determine whether a top-load, front-load or semi-automatic machine fits best.

7-9 kg classFront / top loadInverter motorWash programs
  • Choose capacity based on average laundry load, not only family count.
  • Front-load machines generally use space differently from top-load models.
  • Check cycle choices, spin speed and warranty on the motor.
